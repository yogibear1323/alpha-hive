This previewWhat you can click through

Create account opens a four-step wizard: passphrase, connect a wallet, an optional bot trading account, then risk caps with Demo on and Live off. You then enter a terminal mockup to fund an example address, simulate a deposit, preview a Swap to USDC, and finish on Ready.

There are no real wallet calls, no key generation, and no network calls. Every number is an example.

Step 3 of 4 · Setup wizardOptional bot trading account

Design preview, not live — no key is generated

In the real setup the localhost bridge would generate a new Stellar keypair. The secret would be stored only in bridge/.env, never shown, logged, or committed. Withdrawals would be allowlisted to your connected wallet. This preview does none of that.
