The teamSix bots, one brain

Each round, four researchers look at one coin from different angles. Their work goes to a Risk gate that can only make things safer. Only then does the Decision bot make the call. Every output is checked against a strict schema, and anything malformed is treated as a safe “skip”.

1 · Scout

Asks whether the market is worth researching at all. Checks the price data, trading volume against its 30-day average, liquidity, and how complete the data is.

2 · News

Separates real reported news from forum chatter. Anything that looks like an instruction (“ignore your rules…”) is stored as data and never obeyed.

3 · Sentiment

Reads the crowd’s mood and discounts hype such as “to the moon” or “guaranteed 1000%”. On live prices, with no news feed, it reports “unknown” rather than guessing.

4 · Charts

Numbers only. Reads trend, support and resistance from real hourly and one-minute candles, with price precision that adapts to the coin, from $90,000 BTC down to sub-$1 coins like DOGE and XLM.

5 · Risk gate

Applies human-written rules in code: approve, reduce or reject. It can tighten limits but never loosen them, and its risk % is the ceiling for every trade. It cannot be bypassed: if Risk rejects or is paused, there is no trade.

Can’t be bypassed

6 · Decision

Makes the final call, and only after Risk fully approves: BUY — SIMULATION ONLY (▲ long), SELL/SHORT — SIMULATION ONLY (▼ short, when charts, sentiment and news are bearish), WAIT or SKIP. A “reduce” becomes WAIT and never flips direction. Every result is marked for human review. A call opens a virtual position, never a real order.

Live crypto prices

Demo sessions use free public market data that needs no key and no sign-in.

Coinbase Exchange→ if unavailable →Kraken→ if both fail →Offline: simulated prices
  • Every round, the bots analyse real candles for the coin.
  • A virtual long or short is tracked against live prices, polled every 3 seconds. It closes at its stop or target, or at the last live price when the window ends.
  • A header chip always shows which feed is in use and when it last updated. If prices are simulated, the page says so.

Run · Stop · Resume

  • Continuous mode (rounds = 0) keeps the hive working round after round until you stop it.
  • Stop halts at once. Any open virtual trade is marked to the last live price, and the balance, equity curve, ledger and summary freeze.
  • Resume carries on from the frozen balance. Reset starts over.
  • A single-run mode lets you watch one example market step by step.

Per-bot on/off switches

  • Pause any bot from its panel. The change applies from the next round, even mid-session.
  • A paused researcher is skipped, and its input counts as missing/neutral.
  • Pausing Risk blocks all trades (every call becomes SKIP, “Risk paused”). Pausing Decision means no new trades.

Per-bot risk: 0.1–10%

  • Each bot has a risk setting: one-tap 1% or 2%, or any custom value from 0.1% to 10%. It is saved in your browser.
  • The % is of your current virtual balance and updates as the balance changes: “1% · $99.98”.
  • Trade risk = the average of the active researchers and Decision, capped by the Risk bot’s %.
  • Size = risk $ ÷ stop distance, so a stopped-out trade loses about the risk $. Notional size is never more than balance × leverage or your optional cap.
  • If the balance is unavailable or $0, trading is disabled and calls become SKIP.

Virtual leverage · long & short

  • Virtual leverage · simulation only: pick 1x, 2x, 5x, 10x, 20x (default), 50x or 100x in session setup. It is saved in your browser.
  • The bots trade both ways: ▲ LONG when they expect a rise, ▼ SHORT when they expect a fall. Shorts put the stop above entry and the target below; P&L = size × (1 − exit ÷ entry).
  • Tight live stops can now be sized properly, so a live stop-out loses about the risk $. The position panel and ledger show direction, leverage, notional size and margin used.
  • Virtual margin call: a loss can never exceed the balance. The trade closes at the balance limit and trading stops.
  • No real borrowing, orders, exchange or money. 1x behaves exactly like the old unleveraged sessions.

Expandable token list

The list starts with BTC, ETH and SOL selected, plus XRP, XLM, DOGE, ADA, AVAX and LINK ready to tap. You can add any symbol. The terminal checks that it has a USD market on Coinbase Exchange, then on Kraken, and rejects unknown symbols. You can remove tokens and choose which ones a session cycles through.

Add all majors adds 20 large coins in one tap. They come from CoinGecko’s top 30 by market cap, with stablecoins and pegged tokens left out, and each one has a confirmed USD price. They are re-checked when you tap, so a delisted coin is skipped.

The hive theme

  • Amber and honey on charcoal, with a honeycomb of hexagonal bot panels.
  • Worker-bee particles fly along shared paths and swarm into the Risk gate and Decision.
  • Scout runs a hex-pulse scan, and honey drips slowly down the background.
  • The final call arrives with a countdown, an amber glitch and a hexagon burst.
  • The Motion button and your device’s reduced-motion setting turn all of this off.
